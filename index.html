Current asset values · Plan includes investments and projected property equity. Property debt is held constant.
Projection assumptions

Illustrative—not a forecast. Monthly cash flows use fixed nominal amounts, with no inflation, tax or fees. Contributions are limited to income after expenses; shortfalls draw from investments. Surplus income stays outside the portfolio. Employment and contract income stop at retirement; benefits and other income continue. An income override preserves that source mix. Age changes take effect on your birthday; cash flows are prorated within that month. For 29 February birthdays, Mercury uses 28 February in non-leap years.

Dividend yield
Distribution policy

Return includes dividends. Reinvested dividends remain invested; other distribution policies make dividends available to cover expenses and scheduled contributions. Property market value compounds at its appreciation assumption, then recorded debt is subtracted. Debt is held constant; mortgage repayments, sale costs, improvements and rental income are not modelled. Property equity cannot fund spending. County rates use FHFA’s ten-year nominal house-price history, a regional single-family housing estimate rather than a valuation or forecast for your home. Values can fall. Missing appreciation holds current property value constant and is flagged above. Value change includes investment contributions and withdrawals; expected annual growth excludes them. The growth percentage uses projected net worth; the dividend yield uses investments only.
